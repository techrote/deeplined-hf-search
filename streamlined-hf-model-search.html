Advanced search · model names and cards

Name searches automatically fetch relevant models from the Hub before applying your full query. Card-only searches and regex searches without a name hint scan a broader sample. This is not a full-Hub card index; the explorer filters below do not apply here.

Advanced options · sampling and candidate hints

A hint overrides automatic discovery and searches names only. Leave it empty for automatic discovery. Up to the selected number of candidates are checked; more may exist. For card-only or regex searches, a name hint can narrow the sample but will omit models that mention the term only in their cards.

All terms are required (AND), with or without +. Use -term to exclude, "exact phrase" for adjacent text, ? for exactly one character, and * for any number of characters (including spaces/newlines). Use *{2,5} for 2–5 characters, *{3} for exactly 3, or *{2,} for 2 or more (bounds up to 10,000). Escape operators with a backslash, e.g. \* for a literal star. Matching is case-insensitive and terms may occur anywhere in the text. If both name and card searches are supplied, both must match. Cards include raw README.md and YAML metadata.

Enter patterns without /delimiters/. Flags: i = ignore case, m = multiline anchors, s = dot matches newlines, u = Unicode. Supply at least one pattern; when both are supplied, both must match. Names include author/model. Cards are raw README.md text, including YAML metadata; each name-matching candidate may need one extra request.
